About Secure Map Viewer

Welcome to Secure Map Viewer, your trusted platform for accessing and viewing geographical maps from various government departments and organizations. Our platform provides a secure, view-only environment designed to protect sensitive geographical data while making it accessible to authorized users.

Our Mission

We aim to provide a centralized, secure platform where users can access maps from multiple departments including Police, Railway, PWD, Electricity Board, and more. Our focus is on maintaining the integrity and security of geographical data while ensuring easy access for legitimate purposes.

Key Features

🔒 Secure Viewing

View-only access with advanced security measures to prevent unauthorized downloads and copying.

📂 Multiple Departments

Access maps from various government departments and organizations in one place.

🗂️ Dynamic Categories

Navigate through flexible, hierarchical categories tailored to each department's structure.

📄 Multiple Formats

Support for PDF, KMZ, and KML file formats for comprehensive map viewing.

💬 Request System

Submit requests for specific maps through our forum system.

🎯 User Friendly

Clean, intuitive interface designed for easy navigation and map discovery.

Security & Privacy

We take security seriously. Our platform implements multiple layers of protection including:

  • View-only access preventing downloads and unauthorized copying
  • Disabled right-click and keyboard shortcuts for saving content
  • Protected PDF viewing with restricted permissions
  • Secure file serving with appropriate headers
  • Regular security audits and updates

Who We Serve

Our platform serves government officials, urban planners, researchers, and authorized personnel who need access to geographical maps for planning, analysis, and decision-making purposes. Each user accesses maps within their authorized scope.

Need Specific Maps?

If you're looking for specific maps that aren't currently available on our platform, please use our forum submission system to request them. Our team reviews all requests and works to make relevant maps available.

Contact Information

For inquiries about map purchases, custom requests, or general questions, please visit our contact page. Our team is here to help you with your mapping needs.
